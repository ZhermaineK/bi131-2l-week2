=== ALPHAFOLD 3 REQUIREMENTS VS. LOCAL CAPABILITY ===
- Hardware Requirement: High-end Linux workstation with compatible NVIDIA GPU (CUDA), minimum 64 GB RAM, and ~1 TB disk space for sequence databases.
- Observed Local Environment: Apple Silicon Mac with limited RAM (8 GB) and no CUDA-compatible NVIDIA GPU.
- Conclusion: Full local inference of AlphaFold 3 is NOT feasible or appropriate on this machine. Running CPU-only inference would be prohibitively slow (~100x slower), and local storage cannot house the full 1 TB genetic databases. A local-control and remote-compute design (utilizing AlphaFold DB / AlphaFold Server) is required.
